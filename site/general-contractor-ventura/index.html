California Custom Builders

California Custom Builders provides preconstruction and general contracting for substantial residential and commercial projects in Ventura and throughout Ventura County.

Our work begins by understanding the property, intended scope, design status, budget expectations and schedule. We coordinate with architects, engineers and specialty trades to identify constructability issues and organize a practical plan for the work.

Once construction begins, we manage subcontractors, procurement, inspections, schedule, quality and communication through completion. Minor repairs are also available locally, but our primary focus remains custom homes, additions, ADUs, major remodels and commercial construction.
