California Custom Builders

Ground-up residential construction requires thousands of connected decisions. We organize those decisions early so the design, budget and construction plan support the same goal.

California Custom Builders works with owners and their architects and engineers from early feasibility through final completion. We review constructability, help clarify trade scopes and develop cost information as the plans advance.

During construction, we coordinate subcontractors, procurement, inspections, schedule and quality while keeping the owner informed. The result is a custom residence managed as one complete project rather than a collection of disconnected trades.
