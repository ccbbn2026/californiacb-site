Local knowledge

Building in Camarillo: what to plan for.

HOA architectural review

Many Camarillo HOAs review exterior changes such as additions, rooflines, colors and windows. California law limits how far an HOA can restrict an ADU, but most associations still review its design. We plan the HOA submittal alongside the city permit so one doesn’t hold up the other.

Matching a planned neighborhood

In a planned community, an addition that doesn’t match stands out. We match the roofline, roofing and stucco texture to the existing house, the same approach we used on our Oxnard addition, so the finished home reads as one house from the street.

Garage conversions

Under California law, converting a garage into an ADU generally does not require replacement parking. The real work is underground and in the floor: locating the sewer, running new waste lines under the slab and leveling a sloped garage floor, as we did on our Oxnard garage conversion.

Fire review

Camarillo is served by the Ventura County Fire Protection District, which reviews fire requirements such as sprinklers, access and hydrant distance. Hillside lots near open space can also fall within fire hazard severity zones, which bring ignition-resistant construction requirements.

Questions

Camarillo project FAQ

Can I build an ADU if my Camarillo home is in an HOA?

In most cases, yes. California law limits an HOA’s ability to prohibit or unreasonably restrict ADUs, though the association can still review the design. We plan for both the HOA and the city approval.

Do I need to replace parking if I convert my garage to an ADU?

Generally no. California law does not require replacement parking when a garage is converted into an ADU. We confirm the details for your property during planning.

How long does a garage-to-ADU conversion take?

Our two most recent garage conversions each took about three months of construction, after design and permits were complete.

Is California Custom Builders licensed?

Yes. California Custom Builders holds California contractor license #929421 and is licensed, bonded and insured. You can confirm the license on the Contractors State License Board website.
