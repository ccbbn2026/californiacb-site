Concrete slab testing

RH slab moisture testing.

Flooring failures often start with moisture in the slab. We perform in-situ relative humidity (RH) testing of concrete slabs to ASTM F2170 before flooring is installed, for our own projects and for flooring contractors and owners.

ASTM F2170 RH testing

Probes set in the slab measure relative humidity where it affects the flooring system.

Documented results

A written report of test locations and readings for the flooring installer and manufacturer.

Before flooring replacement

Testing scheduled ahead of flooring work in commercial, public and residential buildings.
