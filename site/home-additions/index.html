Major home additions

Add space without losing what works.

Major home additions in Ventura County, including preconstruction, budgeting, design coordination and complete construction management.

California Custom Builders

A successful addition must work with the existing structure, utilities, circulation and character of the home. We evaluate those connections before construction and manage the work through final completion.

We help owners define the intended space, understand the likely construction implications and establish realistic budget expectations. As plans develop, we coordinate constructability questions and identify work that affects the occupied portions of the residence.

Our team manages structural work, building systems, exterior tie-ins, interior finishes and the many trade transitions required to make the new space feel intentional.
