Local knowledge

Building in Oxnard: what to plan for.

Garage conversions start underground

In a garage conversion, the sewer decides the layout. On our Oxnard garage-to-ADU conversion, we located the sewer and confirmed its depth before demolition, cut the slab and driveway to run new waste lines, and poured a new level slab over the sloped garage floor. Then we built a new roof over the existing walls and reroofed the main house to match.

Additions that look original

On our Oxnard home addition, we opened the existing exterior walls into the new space, tied a new foundation into the old house, matched the roof and the stucco texture, and replaced every window while the walls were open. Construction took four months and was finished just before Thanksgiving.

Working with existing utilities

More house does not always mean bigger utilities, but it has to be checked. On the Oxnard addition, the larger home had to work with the utility sizes already serving the house, so we planned around them from the start instead of finding out at inspection.

Oxnard permits

Building permits in Oxnard are submitted to the city’s Building & Engineering counter at 214 South C Street, and the Planning Division reviews ADU setbacks and size. The city also publishes pre-approved ADU plans. Properties near the beach may be in the coastal zone, which can add a review step.

Oxnard project FAQ

Can you convert my garage into an ADU in Oxnard?

Yes. We have completed a garage-to-ADU conversion in Oxnard with a separate entrance, new sewer lines under the slab, a level floor and a new roof. Construction took about three months.

Does Oxnard have pre-approved ADU plans?

The City of Oxnard publishes pre-approved ADU plans on its website. Whether one fits depends on your lot, setbacks and utilities. We can build from pre-approved plans or from a custom design.

How long did your Oxnard home addition take?

Four months of construction. The project added a primary bedroom and bathroom, expanded the kitchen and living room, and replaced all of the home’s windows.
