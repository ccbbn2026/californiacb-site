California Custom Builders

California Custom Builders serves Ventura with preconstruction, estimating, design coordination and complete general contracting for substantial residential and commercial work.

Ventura’s established neighborhoods, coastal conditions and mix of older and newer properties reward careful existing-condition review and practical preconstruction.

We help owners move from an early idea or developing plan set toward a construction-ready scope. Our process addresses feasibility, budgeting, design-team coordination, permits and procurement before field work begins. During construction, we manage trade sequencing, inspections, quality and communication through completion.
