Local knowledge

Building in Ventura: what to plan for.

Older homes on raised foundations

Many older Ventura homes sit on a raised perimeter foundation with a crawl space rather than a slab. An addition has to meet that floor height so the new rooms connect without a step. On our Ventura addition with a roof deck, we built the new foundation to the height of the existing one, then matched the windows, the stucco and the coved doorways inside.

Electrical service in older houses

Adding a bedroom, bathroom or kitchen space often pushes an older electrical service past what it was built for. We look at the panel and service early so an upgrade is in the budget from the start, as it was on the Ventura addition, rather than a surprise during construction.

Coastal and hillside lots

Properties in the coastal zone can need coastal review before a building permit is issued, and hillside lots raise grading, drainage and access questions. We check for both at the start, because they tend to affect the schedule more than the construction itself.

City or county permits

Projects inside city limits are permitted by the City of Ventura, with fire review by the city’s own fire department. Nearby properties outside city limits go through Ventura County instead. Knowing which agency has your property is the first step toward a realistic schedule.

Ventura project FAQ

Do you build ADUs and home additions in Ventura?

Yes. We build detached ADUs, garage-to-ADU conversions, home additions and custom homes in Ventura, along with major remodels and commercial work. Our recent Ventura project added a dining room, bedroom and bathroom with a roof deck and fireplace on top.

How long does a home addition take?

It depends on size and scope. A recent addition of ours, a new primary suite with an expanded kitchen and living room in Oxnard, took four months of construction. Design, engineering and permits come before that, and their timing depends on the city and the property.

Where is California Custom Builders located?

We are based in Ventura and work throughout Ventura County, plus Santa Barbara, Goleta, Carpinteria, Malibu, Westlake Village, Agoura Hills and West Los Angeles.
