Whole-home remodeling and major renovations throughout Ventura County with preconstruction and complete general contracting.

California Custom Builders

Major remodeling is most successful when structure, systems, layout and finishes are planned together. We organize the work as one coordinated construction program.

We work with owners and design professionals to define the renovation scope, assess existing conditions and establish priorities. Preconstruction budgeting helps distinguish essential work from optional upgrades before demolition exposes the project to unnecessary uncertainty.

During construction, we coordinate structural modifications, mechanical and electrical systems, kitchens, bathrooms, finishes and exterior connections while maintaining a clear schedule and communication process.
