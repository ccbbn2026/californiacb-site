Interior construction ready for business.

Tenant improvements, office renovations and commercial interior buildouts in Ventura County.

California Custom Builders

A tenant improvement must translate plans and operational needs into a coordinated interior buildout. We manage the details that connect the space, trades and schedule.

We support office, retail and other commercial interior projects with estimating, constructability review and trade coordination. Early planning helps clarify landlord requirements, existing conditions, finish selections and work that may affect occupied areas.

Construction management includes demolition, framing, building systems, doors, ceilings, flooring, millwork, finishes and closeout as required by the approved scope.
