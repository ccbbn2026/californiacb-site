Local knowledge

Building in Ojai: what to plan for.

Fire-resistant construction

Much of the land around Ojai is mapped in fire hazard severity zones. New construction there must meet California’s wildland-urban interface building standards, which cover ignition-resistant exterior materials, vents, eaves, decks and windows. These requirements shape the design and budget, so we address them at the start.

Utilities sized for the house

On our Ojai fire rebuild, the electrical service was relocated to the point closest to the utility pole, the gas line was upsized for the larger home, and the water line was upsized to supply a new fire sprinkler system. We coordinated those changes with the service providers early.

Keeping what survived

On the same rebuild, the original slab was kept and a new slab section was added for the larger floor plan. The new garage needed over-excavation and compaction testing before its slab could be poured, which we scheduled so the pour wasn’t held up.

City or county

Properties inside city limits are permitted by the City of Ojai, which has published a ready-to-use plan set for a 499-square-foot, one-bedroom detached ADU. Communities such as Meiners Oaks, Mira Monte and Oak View are unincorporated and go through Ventura County instead. Fire review in both is handled by the Ventura County Fire Protection District.

Questions

Ojai project FAQ

Do you handle fire rebuilds in Ojai?

Yes. Our Thomas Fire rebuild in Ojai kept the original slab, added 800 square feet and a three-car garage, upsized the utilities for a fire sprinkler system and was completed in five months of construction.

Does a new ADU in Ojai need fire sprinklers?

Under state law, an ADU generally does not need sprinklers if they aren’t required for the main house. Local fire rules can still require them, for example in very high fire hazard severity zones. We confirm the requirement for your property during planning.

Do you work in Meiners Oaks, Mira Monte and Oak View?

Yes. We work throughout the Ojai Valley, including unincorporated communities permitted by Ventura County.

Is California Custom Builders licensed?

Yes. California Custom Builders holds California contractor license #929421 and is licensed, bonded and insured. You can confirm the license on the Contractors State License Board website.
