# Thomas Fire Rebuild: Ojai
Rebuilding a family home larger than before, on the foundation that survived.

## Project facts
- Location: Ojai, CA
- Completed: 2019
- Type: Thomas Fire rebuild, single-family home
- Size: 1,600 sf before, 2,400 sf after, plus a new three-car garage
- Construction time: 5 months
- Our role: General contractor, including coordination of finish trades the owner handled themselves

## The project
After the original home was lost in the Thomas Fire, the owners chose to rebuild larger rather than replace what they had. The new home grew from 1,600 to 2,400 square feet and added a three-car garage, which meant joining new construction to the existing foundation and bringing the utilities up to what a larger home needs.

## The challenges
- Keeping the original slab. The existing slab was kept, and a new slab section was added to carry the larger floor plan, so new and existing concrete had to work together as one foundation.
- Garage soil conditions. The new garage slab required over-excavation and compaction testing before concrete could be placed.
- Utilities sized for a bigger house. The electrical service was relocated to the point closest to the utility pole, the gas line was upsized for the larger home, and the water line was upsized to supply a new fire sprinkler system.
- Owner-performed finish work. The owners wanted to handle certain finish trades themselves, which meant fitting their work into the overall schedule.

## How we handled it
We planned the foundation work around what could be saved, combining the original slab with the new section and the garage slab. We scheduled the soils work and compaction testing so the garage pour wasn't held up, and coordinated the utility upgrades with the service providers early. For the finish work the owners took on, we provided the scheduling and coordination, so their trades and ours moved through the house in the right order.

## The result
A 2,400-square-foot home with a three-car garage, completed in five months and rebuilt on the property the family already knew, with utilities sized for the house it is now.
