# Home Addition with Roof Deck: Ventura, 2025–26
A dining room, bedroom and bathroom added to an older home, with a roof deck and outdoor fireplace on top, built to look original.

## The project
The owners wanted more room without changing the character of their home: a dining room, a bedroom and a bathroom, plus a roof deck on top of the addition with its own fireplace. The goal was an addition that reads as part of the original house, inside and out.

## The challenges
- Matching a raised foundation. The new foundation had to be built to the height of the existing raised foundation so the floors line up.
- A usable roof deck. The addition's roof had to work as an outdoor living space, with parapet walls, a waterproof deck surface and a fireplace.
- Matching original details. Windows, stucco and the interior cove doorways and ceiling corners all had to match the existing home.

## How we handled it
- Foundation: built to match the existing raised foundation height.
- Roof deck: parapet walls capped with stone wall caps, Tufflex deck coating, and a fireplace on the deck.
- Entry: a redwood overhang over the entry.
- Exterior: windows matched to the original windows; stucco matched to the existing.
- Interior: cove doorways and coved ceiling corners to match the existing home.
- Electrical: panel upgraded.

## The result
A dining room, bedroom and bathroom that blend into the original house, topped by a roof deck with a fireplace.
