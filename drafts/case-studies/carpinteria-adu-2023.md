# Garage-to-ADU Conversion: Carpinteria, 2023
A garage turned into a bright, vaulted living space, keeping the parts of the original structure worth keeping.

## Project facts
- Location: Carpinteria, CA
- Completed: 2023, done by Christmas
- Type: Garage-to-ADU conversion
- Construction time: 3 months
- Our role: General contractor

## The project
The owners wanted their garage converted into an accessory dwelling unit. Rather than tear the structure down to the slab, we kept what worked: the existing roof, the garage ridge beam and the garage door beam all stayed and became part of the new unit, which gave the ADU vaulted ceilings and saved the cost of rebuilding what was already sound.

## The challenges
- Plumbing under an existing slab. The existing slab had to be cut to run the new sewer lines.
- A sloped garage floor. New concrete was poured over the existing slab to level the floor.
- Turning a garage into a home without a new roof. With the existing roof staying in place, the rafters had to be insulated to living-space standards.
- Making the converted garage blend in. The new exterior stucco had to match the existing stucco.

## How we handled it
- Interior demolition: all existing drywall and flooring removed down to the structure.
- Slab: cut for the new sewer lines, then new concrete poured over the existing slab to level the floor.
- Structure: the existing garage door beam was reused as part of the new wall, and the garage ridge beam was kept for its look and to allow vaulted ceilings.
- Insulation: Icynene spray foam at the rafters to reach R-38.
- Exterior: new stucco matched to the existing; the existing roof remained.
- Utilities: electrical panel upgraded; water line upgraded.

Finishes:
- White birch cabinets
- Quartz countertops
- Polished concrete floors
- Pocket door
- Tile floor in the bathroom

## The result
A vaulted, well-insulated ADU with polished concrete floors and a white kitchen, built inside the old garage in three months and finished by Christmas.
