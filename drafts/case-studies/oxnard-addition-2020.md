# Home Addition: Oxnard
A new primary suite and a larger kitchen and living room, finished in time for Thanksgiving.

## Project facts
- Location: Oxnard, CA
- Completed: 2020, just before Thanksgiving
- Type: Home addition and interior remodel
- Scope: New primary bedroom and bathroom; expanded living room and kitchen; whole-house window replacement
- Size: [ask Juan]
- Construction time: 4 months
- Our role: General contractor, all aspects of construction

## The project
The owners wanted a primary bedroom and bathroom of their own, plus more room where the family spends its time: a bigger living room and kitchen. That meant opening up the existing house rather than building a separate wing, so the new space had to look and feel like it had always been there.

## The challenges
- Opening the existing house. Existing exterior walls had to be demolished to connect the addition to the living room and kitchen, while keeping the rest of the home intact.
- A new foundation tied to the old house. The addition needed its own new foundation, built to join the existing structure.
- Making the addition disappear. The new roof had to match the existing roof, and the stucco texture had to match the original so the addition wouldn't stand out from the street.
- Working within the existing utilities. The larger home had to be served by the same utility sizes the house already had.

## How we handled it
We handled every part of the construction, from demolishing the exterior walls and pouring the new foundation through framing, roofing, stucco and interior finishes. The roof was built to match the existing roof, and the stucco texture was matched to the original. While the walls were open, all of the home's old windows were replaced with new-construction windows, so the whole house got the upgrade, not just the addition.

Inside, the finishes were chosen to tie the new and existing rooms together:
- Maple cabinets
- Quartz countertops
- Tile flooring
- Crown molding
- New double doors into the primary bedroom

## The result
The family moved into a new primary suite with a larger kitchen and living room, finished in four months, just before Thanksgiving.
