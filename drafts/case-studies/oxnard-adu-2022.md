# Garage-to-ADU Conversion: Oxnard, 2022
Turning an attached garage into a separate living unit, starting with what was under the slab.

## Project facts
- Location: Oxnard, CA
- Completed: 2022
- Type: Garage-to-ADU conversion
- Size / layout: [ask Juan]
- Construction time: 3 months
- Our role: General contractor, including all finish trades

## The project
The owners wanted to turn their garage into an accessory dwelling unit with its own separate entrance. A garage is built to park cars, not to live in, so most of what makes a conversion work happens before anything new goes up: finding the sewer, getting waste lines under the slab, leveling the floor and putting a real roof over the space.

## The challenges
- Finding the sewer first. Before anything else, we had to locate the existing sewer line and confirm how deep it was, because that decides whether the new bathroom and kitchen can drain to it and how.
- Extensive demolition. The conversion required substantial demo, including removing the garage roof.
- Getting waste lines under the slab. The existing garage slab had to be cut to run the new sewer and waste lines.
- Cutting the driveway without scarring it. The existing driveway had to be cut as well, and the cut was laid out with the finished look of the property in mind.
- A sloped garage floor. Garage slabs are built to slope; a new slab was poured over the existing one to give the ADU a level floor.

## How we handled it
We started underground, locating the sewer and planning the waste-line routes before demolition. We reused the existing garage walls and built a new roof over them, then reroofed the entire main house along with the ADU so both roofs match. The electrical panel and the water line were upgraded to serve the added unit. We handled all of the finish trades ourselves, through to a completed unit with its own entrance.

Inside, the unit was finished with:
- Birch cabinets
- Quartz countertops
- LVP (luxury vinyl plank) flooring
- Shower enclosure
- Six-panel interior doors
- Two-tone interior paint

## The result
A self-contained ADU with a separate entrance, completed in three months and built inside the footprint of the old garage, with a level floor, a new roof that matches a newly reroofed main house, and upgraded electrical and water service.
